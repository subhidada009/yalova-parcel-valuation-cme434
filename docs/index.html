SPATIAL VALIDATION

How well does the map predict?

Thirteen dated parcels in Armutlu, Altınova and Merkez cover six spatial blocks. Five-fold testing held out whole blocks. All three models scored below zero on R², so estimates for individual purchases carry substantial uncertainty.

METHODS & DATA

Download the map data

The model combines parcel attributes, seven accessibility distances and three Sentinel-2 spectral indices. The grid fixes the parcel attributes and varies the location. Index 100 equals the province-wide median prediction.
